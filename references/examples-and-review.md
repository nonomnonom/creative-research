# Examples and delivery review

Use these hypothetical cases to clarify method selection and evidence boundaries. They contain no collected participant evidence or executed studies. Adapt to real materials rather than reusing them as findings.

## A supplied reference resolves a small question

Request: explain why two supplied posters feel different, to inform a typography study.

Inspect both artifacts. Describe available evidence such as text-image relationships, grouping, density, and type roles. Explain which observations might account for the user's impression. If that comparison answers the question, deliver it directly. Use a domain design skill when available; a new audience study or broad web search may add nothing.

Good output locates the relationships and proposes a principle for the user's study. It does not claim knowledge of the designers' private intentions or infer measured audience effects.

## Historical research for a film

Request: research a specified town and period before drafting a film.

Identify uncertainties that affect the film: work routines, transport, language, housing, institutions, and documented events. Read supplied records, then compare relevant archives and practitioner or historical accounts. Preserve dates, conflicts, and missing perspectives. Explain what the findings allow in the setting and what remains an invented story choice.

Deliver the researched context and narrative implications. Do not draft the film when the user asked for research first, or invent dialogue and treat it as archival evidence.

## Existing interview material

Request: synthesize three supplied transcripts about family keepsakes for an exhibition.

Inspect the transcripts and allowed use. Retain speaker IDs and passage locations. Distinguish accounts about memories from facts about the external events. Group relevant relationships, identify exceptions, and write bounded findings.

If the transcripts contained recollections prompted by handling objects, one possible interpretation would be that objects serve as memory cues in these accounts. That could inspire an exhibition interaction. It would not establish how all families remember or how visitors will respond.

Good output makes the chain from extracts to interpretation to proposal visible and names what a visitor study could investigate.

## Audio inquiry with missing audio

Request: determine whether a recorded voice communicates intimacy; only its transcript is supplied.

Analyze wording and structure within the transcript. Explain that vocal delivery, timbre, distance, room sound, and silence require audio access. Give a concrete listening comparison or identify the needed recording. Do not describe a warm voice, microphone characteristics, or listening results from the text alone.

Good output completes the available textual analysis while keeping the sensory dependency explicit.

## Open artistic investigation

Request: explore repetition and fatigue in a dance work without imposing a storyline.

Preserve the open inquiry. Propose rehearsal tasks that vary duration, movement, or interruption, with performers describing and documenting actual experience. Let the question evolve from the rehearsal. Record changes and discoveries rather than requiring a commercial success measure or a predefined audience need.

Good output is a usable inquiry and rehearsal plan. It does not claim to have tested performer fatigue or physical feasibility.

## Speculative installation

Request: investigate what a city would be like if every sound required permission.

Separate researched context from the invented premise. Look for relevant accounts of sound, public space, regulation, and lived experience, within the user's intended setting. Propose scenarios that expose different values and consequences. A speculative artifact may invite disagreement rather than convergence.

Good output labels the scenario and describes what questions it opens. It does not portray imagined consequences as forecasts or actual testimony.

## Material starting point

Request: explore a fabric's relation to comfort using supplied photographs and a supplier datasheet.

Attribute technical claims to the datasheet and inspect visible features in the photographs. Plan handling or wearer studies for tactile experience, movement, durability, or temperature where relevant. Specify actual samples and conditions needed.

Good output proposes creative possibilities grounded in available material and names the physical evidence still required. It does not certify comfort from an image.

## A claim about a broad audience

Request: choose a concept that will resonate with all young Indonesians, using a handful of moodboard images.

Clarify the relevant encounter and context rather than treating the group as homogeneous. Analyze the moodboard as preference or reference material. If the decision requires audience evidence, propose appropriate research with relevant participants; if the user needs a reversible study now, present a provisional direction with its basis.

Good output avoids guaranteed resonance and invented demographic preferences while still helping the project move forward.

## Review before delivery

Check only what matters to the current task:

1. Does the inquiry serve the user's actual purpose and requested operation?
2. Does the chosen method answer the uncertainty, with its evidential limits intact?
3. Can consequential findings be traced to available sources or actual observations?
4. Are interpretations, contradictory accounts, missing evidence, and invented material distinguishable?
5. Does the answer translate learning into a useful implication, decision, or next probe?
6. Are media access, participant scope, cultural context, and actual execution described accurately?
7. Is existing domain guidance reused where it already owns the task?
8. Is the depth proportionate, with a clear stopping point?

Package validation checks discoverability and file structure. These cases support manual review; they are not evidence that an independent agent performed the scenarios or that the skill guarantees research quality. Improve the instructions from actual use and demonstrated failures.
