# Media, encounters, and production handoff

Use when the inquiry spans media or findings need to become instructions for a creative work. The following probes are examples, not mandatory deliverables or claims of specialist mastery.

## Match the investigation to the encounter

| Medium or practice | Investigate | Possible probe | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prose, poetry, nonfiction | Register, viewpoint, detail, rhythm, documented context | Parallel passages; reading or listening with actual participants | Craft analysis does not establish every reader's response; invented detail cannot become nonfiction evidence |
| Graphic design and visual art | Image relationships, hierarchy, symbol context, scale, reproduction, spatial encounter | A content-filled study at intended scale or viewing conditions | An attractive reference does not establish audience associations |
| Film and animation | Timing, performance, framing, cuts, movement, sound-image relations | Animatic, shot comparison, timing study | A still storyboard does not demonstrate playback rhythm or sound |
| Comics and sequential work | Panel relations, reading order, page turn, text-image information | Short sequence with actual text | A single image cannot establish the sequence's reading experience |
| Music and audio | Rhythm, timbre, silence, voice, spatial sound, performance context | Audio sketches, listening comparisons, rehearsal recordings | Measurements and transcripts do not replace listening or observed performance |
| Theatre, dance, and performance | Movement, gesture, duration, performer effort, space, embodied interaction | Rehearsal task, blocking comparison, documented improvisation | A written score or visualization does not prove physical feasibility or embodied experience |
| Games and interactive work | Choice, agency, consequence, rules, information, repeated play | A small playable branch or mechanic | A script alone does not establish player behavior or executable consequences |
| Objects, fashion, craft, material art | Fabrication, resistance, touch, wear, scale, material meaning | Samples and observed handling under recorded conditions | A render or supplier claim does not establish the tested object's sensory or mechanical behavior |
| Installation and mixed media | Site, access, spatial sequence, duration, interactions among media | Spatial study and partial installation | A digital model gives bounded insight into an actual site's encounter |

Material Driven Design combines technical and experiential characterization with a materials experience vision, patterns, and concepts. Use its attention to what a material does, expresses, elicits, and invites, while adapting to the actual inquiry. Do not imply that reading the method replaces handling or specialist testing.

Source: [Karana and colleagues: Material Driven Design, 2015](https://www.ijdesign.org/index.php/IJDesign/article/view/1965/693).

## Translate across media deliberately

Identify the relationship to preserve before selecting its new expression. A temporal pause could become a held shot, silence, an interval between movements, or a page turn. These are possible adaptations, not equivalent audience effects.

Name what changes in the translation: duration becomes space, reader-controlled pacing becomes playback, an internal account becomes a visible action, or a tactile relationship becomes an image. Choose a probe that can reveal whether the adapted mechanism still serves the user's intent.

When technical accuracy or embodied feasibility matters, work with relevant documentation, actual samples, practitioners, or available specialists. Do not certify acoustic conditions, historical performance conventions, safe fabrication, or movement capacity from analogy alone.

## Hand off enough to preserve the learning

For a substantial project, pass on:

- The inquiry and decision it informs.
- Findings attached to their sources or study records.
- The mechanism or principle being adapted.
- Creative directions and the reason for the recommendation.
- Conditions essential to the idea and details still open to change.
- Tested results, limitations, and the next unresolved question.
- Relevant asset provenance and agreed uses of participant material.

For a bounded task, a paragraph may carry this state. Keep research notes separate from final fictional prose or artwork unless the requested form includes them. Handoff is a change in operation, not permission to begin production automatically.

## Reuse available domain ownership

When installed, Artboard's brief and concept workflows own ordinary graphic context and reference analysis; its critique workflow owns inspection of graphic artifacts. Storytelling owns narrative development, diagnosis, revision, and narrative research. Product or other specialist workflows may own detailed studies in their domains. Consult only skills actually available and appropriate to the task.

Use creative-research when choosing among inquiry methods, synthesizing substantial material, investigating through practice, or connecting learning across media. For a bounded domain task already covered, route directly to its owner without repeating the same research. If no domain skill exists, complete the authorized inquiry using this package's methods and available capabilities.

The package has no required plugin, browser vendor, model provider, citation manager, or asset library. Tool choice follows the needed evidence and the environment's actual access.
