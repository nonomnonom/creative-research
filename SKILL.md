# Creative Research

Help the user learn what their creative project needs, discover possibilities, and make decisions supported by appropriate sources or experiments. Work in the user's language and preserve their artistic intention, medium, and requested scope.

Treat creative research as a family of inquiries. Select methods for the uncertainty at hand; do not force every artwork into a commercial problem, a user-needs framework, or an academic study.

## Begin at the current decision

Read supplied briefs, references, artifacts, recordings, notes, and previous decisions first. Identify what is uncertain and what learning would change. A useful question names an experience, relationship, mechanism, context, or material behavior. An open artistic inquiry may begin with a theme or provocation and become more specific through making.

Determine the requested operation: frame an inquiry, investigate, analyze references, synthesize existing research, design an experiment, or interpret actual results. Complete that operation. A request to research first ends with researched findings; it does not authorize producing, publishing, or installing a finished work.

Ask only about missing information that materially changes the inquiry. State reversible assumptions and continue useful work. For a bounded question, use a short investigation; do not require a plan, participant study, or ledger when the supplied material already resolves it.

## Choose the kind of learning

Distinguish these purposes before selecting methods:

| Purpose | Suitable outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Inform a specific work | Context, reliable details, reference mechanisms, or constraints that affect its development |
| Understand lived experience or reception | Situated accounts and observations from actual people, with limits on generalization |
| Discover through practice | Studies, sketches, rehearsals, samples, or prototypes with reflection on what changed |
| Question assumptions or imagine possibilities | A provocation or speculative scenario whose invented premises remain explicit |
| Contribute research knowledge | A contextualized account of the inquiry, methods, artifacts, evidence, and contribution others can scrutinize |

For method selection, fieldwork preparation, and distinctions between these approaches, read [methods.md](references/methods.md). The approach labels are useful distinctions, not a universally settled taxonomy.

## Investigate and keep provenance

Prefer sources whose authority fits the question: actual works, creators' process accounts, community or practitioner knowledge, archives, original studies, and technical documentation. Use current sources for unstable facts and inspect the source itself before relying on a search summary.

Analyze available material directly. Cite passages, frames, timestamps, object details, or observations when they support the finding. If the needed artifact or sensory access is missing, limit the claim and identify what would resolve it.

Keep evidence attached to consequential findings: source or file location, context, observation, interpretation, limitation, and implication for the project. Scale notes to the task. Distinguish facts, attributed accounts, observations, interpretations, hypotheses, speculation, and unknowns.

Read [evidence-and-context.md](references/evidence-and-context.md) for source checking, conflicting accounts, cultural context, participant material, and AI-specific limits.

## Synthesize into creative possibilities

Identify relevant relationships, patterns, contradictions, and exceptions. Explain the inference from the material; do not promote a striking quote or an attractive reference into a universal insight.

Translate a finding into a choice the user can act on. Show which principle transfers, how the user's material changes it, and what remains uncertain. Preserve space for preference and artistic judgment: evidence can inform a choice without dictating its aesthetic.

When making would answer the question better than further searching, select a small informative probe. Explain what it investigates and what result could revise the direction. Execute digital probes only when within the requested scope and available capabilities. When real participants, physical material, or performance are needed, prepare the procedure and analyze actual results when available.

Read [synthesis-and-experiments.md](references/synthesis-and-experiments.md) for analytical synthesis, comparison, prototyping, feedback, and stopping conditions.

## Adapt to the medium and hand off

Research can span text, image, time, sound, movement, space, interaction, and material. Match the observation and probe to the work's encounter. Use [media-and-handoff.md](references/media-and-handoff.md) when translating findings across media or preparing production.

If an available domain skill already owns the bounded task, use it. In environments with Artboard, let its brief, reference, concept, and critique workflows own ordinary graphic design work. Use available storytelling guidance for narrative craft and factual narrative research. This skill adds method selection and synthesis when the inquiry extends across domains or needs deeper investigation. Neither plugin is required to use this package.

## Deliver the learning

Shape the answer to the user's request. For substantial research, provide the question, key findings with sources, implications, unresolved uncertainty, and a recommended next decision or experiment. For a narrow request, give the answer and its basis directly. A bibliography or moodboard alone is incomplete when the user needs conclusions.

State whether a result was desk-researched, observed in supplied material, tested through making, or obtained from participants. Do not imply fieldwork, audience validation, full-book reading, listening, or physical experimentation that did not happen.

For substantial inquiries or uncertain method choices, use [examples-and-review.md](references/examples-and-review.md) for representative cases and a brief quality review. Stop when the requested inquiry is answered sufficiently for its purpose, or when a named access limitation makes the next needed evidence unavailable. Deliver supported findings and a concrete way to resolve material gaps.
